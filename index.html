About Le Nam Hai Pham

Le Nam Hai Pham received the bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering from the University of Technology, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam (Dai Hoc Bach Khoa HCM) in 2018, the M.Sc. and Ph.D degree in electrical power engineering from the University of South-Eastern Norway, in 2022 and 2025, respectively.

He was an Electrical Engineer with Space Engineering, a power consultant company in Vietnam, from 2017 to 2020. Currently, he is a Project Manager at Hitachi Energy Norway, handling critical Oil & Gas projects as well as advanced transformer substation deployment projects across Norway.

His core research interests encompass dynamic power systems, cyber-physical energy and power systems, real-time control applications, and protection infrastructure in smart grids.
