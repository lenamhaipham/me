About Le Nam Hai Pham

I am a PMP-certified professional and hold a Ph.D. in Electrical Power Engineering, with a specialization in the digital transformation of the energy sector. I combine academic expertise with industry experience across the oil and gas sector, digital substations, and renewable energy projects.

My professional background includes:

  • Project management for transformer substations, oil and gas, power, and energy projects
  • Specialization in power system simulation and modelling
  • Extensive expertise in power system and protection philosophy, as well as power system optimization
  • Strong skills in real-time simulation and hardware-in-the-loop testing
  • Hands-on experience with laboratory-scale experimental setups focused on digital substations and renewable energy technologies
